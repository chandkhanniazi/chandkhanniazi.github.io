The Journey

Built. Surrendered. Rebuilt.

☾

  1. Childhood

    Often sick, and drawn inward

    In childhood, I often stayed sick. I was foody also. And even then, there was a spiritual side to that phase.

  2. Age 13–14

    The first steps

    Slowly, I began simple exercises — pushups, situps, squats. Nothing more than a boy, a floor, and repetition.

  3. Age 15–16

    The home gym

    I started training properly: a small home gym, basic equipment, and one simple goal — to build a strong natural physique.

  4. Age ~15–19

    The natural peak

    I reached 84 kg (75.6 kg lean body mass) at 10% body fat. A mesomorphic 6′3″ frame can reach 90–95 kg at 10% naturally — but 84 kg was enough and sustainable for me in my teenage years. I was satisfied.

  5. Age 19–22 · BS-IT years

    Holding the line

    Through my BS-IT, I maintained the physique — training, studying, steady.

  6. The Turning Point · ~2 years

    The world, or Him

    In the last semester of my BS-IT, I was fully prepared for my fitness career and my CS career. Everything was lined up in front of me. And then a test came — one of those silent tests that decide the direction of a life: was it going to be the world… or Him? And I chose Him.

    I returned every bit of rizq I had earned, walked away from my plans, and stepped into complete Gosha-Nasheni. I let go of the world entirely — where the world has no voice, where a person is rebuilt only by His Hand. Day and night became one. Time disappeared. I disappeared. My health collapsed. The intensity of muraqbah and spiritual pressure brought weakness, dryness so strong I could hardly blink, pain in every joint. I moved like an old, fragile man. This state lasted years — quiet, hidden, and purely between me and Him.

  7. The Rebuild · Alhamdulillah

    Lifted back up, step by step

    When that phase ended, when the time of rebuilding came — again it was only He who lifted me back up. With His help, I started rebuilding myself: from 90.0 kg at 21.5% body fat, down to 76.9 kg at 8.6% — then a disciplined bulk. Every week of it is logged below.
