Common Questions

Before you ask on WhatsApp

Can I train at home, without a gym?
Yes. Coaching is both gym-based and home-based — your workout plan is built around the equipment you actually have.
Is the coaching online? Where are you located?
Coaching is 1:1 and fully online through WhatsApp — you can enroll from anywhere.
Is everything really natural?
Yes — 100% natural. Steroids are never part of this coaching. Supplements may be suggested where useful, but supplements support nutrition — they are not medicines.
Can teenagers and students enroll?
Yes. The Student plan is designed for students and beginners — and the installment option makes it easier to start.
Can I pay in installments?
The Student plan can be paid monthly: 3,500 × 3 (total 10,500 PKR). One-time payment: 10,000 PKR. Standard and Premium plans are paid in full.
I have questions about my own case — can I ask on WhatsApp?
Personal case questions are answered in the 30-minute consultation (2,000 PKR — adjusted into your coaching fee if you enroll). This keeps the guidance serious and focused.
